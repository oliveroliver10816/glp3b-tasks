Links and buttons: nothing is cross-wired

  • Every page opens on mobile (390px) and desktop (1440px): HTTP 200, no console errors, no sideways scrolling, on all 160 pages.
  • Every buy button on every page points at its own country's redirect (/de/go/ on German pages, /fr/go/ on French pages …). 1040 of 1040 — zero exceptions. No page links to the merchant or the network directly.
  • Internal links stay inside the country. Menu, related pages and footer legal links never leave /xx/. The only links to other countries are the 9 country names in the footer switcher, on every page, by design.

The short header/hero/sticky buttons say only "Order" / "Order now" (they do not name the shop).
